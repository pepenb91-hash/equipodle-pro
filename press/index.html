Description

Every day at midnight Rondo sets three challenges, the same for every player:

  • Teams: guess the mystery club from Europe's top five leagues (96 clubs, 2026-27 season). Every guess reveals seven clues: league, titles, kit colour, squad value, years in the top flight, kit brand and stadium.
  • Legends: recognise a football legend from a blurred photo that sharpens with every miss.
  • Decade: put five moments of football history (1950–2026) in chronological order.

Players keep streaks, share a Wordle-style colour grid and can challenge friends with a link. Most football guessing games are about players; Rondo is built around clubs.
