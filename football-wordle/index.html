Daily football Wordle

Rondo is a daily football game in the style of Wordle: every day there's a mystery club to guess, a legend hidden behind a blurred photo and five moments of football history to put in order. Free, no signup and no ads.

Play now

The 3 modes

⚽ Teams

Guess the mystery club from Europe's top 5 leagues. Every guess gives you 7 clues: league, titles, kit colour, squad value, years in the top flight, kit brand and stadium.

⭐ Legends

A football legend in a blurred photo. You have 10 attempts and the photo gets sharper with every miss. After 5 misses you'll see their birth year.

📅 Decade

Put 5 moments of football history in order, from oldest to newest: transfers, trophies, debuts, retirements and records. You have 3 attempts.

How to play Teams

Type any club from the Premier League, LaLiga, Serie A, Bundesliga or Ligue 1. Each clue is coloured:

Green: matches the mystery club Yellow: you're close Red: no match

For number clues (titles, squad value, years in the top flight and stadium) an arrow tells you whether the mystery club has more or less. Not sure where to start? Roll the dice and Rondo picks a first club for you.

Streaks and challenges

Each mode has its own streak: your ball evolves at 3, 5, 10, 20, 50, 100 and 200 wins in a row. When you finish, share your colour grid on WhatsApp or social media with a challenge link: your friends will see your score and can try to beat it.

FAQ

Is Rondo free?

Yes. It's free, with no signup and no ads.

When does the puzzle change?

Every day at midnight there's a new club, a new legend and 5 new history events, the same for every player.

Which leagues are the clubs from?

From Europe's top 5 leagues in the 2026-27 season: Premier League, LaLiga, Serie A, Bundesliga and Ligue 1 (96 clubs).

Can I play on my phone?

Yes, and you can add Rondo to your home screen to open it like an app.

Where can I see yesterday's answer?

On Rondo's answers page. We only show finished days, never today's puzzle.
